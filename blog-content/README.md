# Blog content — Axiora Labs

Four publish-ready articles, plus everything WordPress asks for when you create the post.

Each `.html` file is the **article body only** — no `<html>`, no `<head>`, no title heading. Paste it
straight into WordPress and the theme supplies the title, byline, hero and styling.

---

## How to publish one

1. **Posts → Add New**
2. Paste the **title** from the table below into the title field.
3. Switch the editor to **Code editor** (⋮ menu top-right → *Code editor*, or `Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M`).
   *Alternatively:* stay in the visual editor and add a single **Custom HTML** block.
4. Paste the contents of the `.html` file.
5. Set the **URL slug**, **category**, **tags** and **excerpt** from the table below
   (Excerpt lives under *Post → Excerpt* in the sidebar; if you don't see it, enable it under
   ⋮ → *Preferences → Panels*).
6. Set a **featured image** — see the image notes at the bottom.
7. Publish.

> Do **not** re-type the article title as an `<h1>` inside the body. The template renders it.
> The body starts at `<h2>`, which keeps the heading hierarchy correct for search engines.

## Categories to create first

**Posts → Categories.** Create these two before publishing, so posts don't land in *Uncategorized*:

- **AI Strategy** — slug `ai-strategy`
- **Industry Insights** — slug `industry-insights`

Two more worth adding as the blog grows: **Engineering** (technical, implementation-level pieces)
and **Company** (announcements, hiring, product news). Keep the total under about six — more
categories than that stops helping readers navigate.

## Featured images

Every post needs one; the listing page and the article hero both use it.

- **Size:** 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Anything wider than 1600 is wasted weight.
- **Format:** JPG for photography, PNG for anything with flat colour or text.
- **Weight:** keep under 250 KB — compress before uploading.
- **Alt text:** always fill it in. It's an accessibility requirement and it's read by search engines.

The brand palette for any custom graphics is in the main README: primary `#3c59e7`, deep blue
`#1a4bc5`, navy `#131f4e`, violet `#6b7cef`. Headings are Outfit, body is Inter.

If you don't have imagery ready, the article template falls back to a branded gradient panel rather
than breaking — so a missing image degrades gracefully, but do add them.

## House style used in these articles

Worth matching in anything written later:

- **British spelling** (organisation, optimise, recognised) — consistent with the rest of the site.
- **Specific over superlative.** No "revolutionary", "cutting-edge" or "game-changing".
- **No invented numbers.** Nothing in these four articles cites a statistic, a client or a result,
  because none could be verified. Every claim is either a reasoned argument or an observed pattern.
  Please keep it that way unless you can point to the source — a single unverifiable statistic
  undermines an otherwise credible piece.
- **Short paragraphs**, two to four sentences. Long paragraphs are not read on screens.
- **The reader is senior and busy.** They are assumed to know their own business and to be sceptical
  of vendors. Nothing is explained condescendingly and nothing is oversold.
- **Every article ends with one clear next step**, not three competing ones.
