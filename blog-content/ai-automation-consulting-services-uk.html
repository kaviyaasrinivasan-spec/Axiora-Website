What Is AI Automation Consulting?

AI Automation Consulting refers to the discipline of identifying, analysing, prioritising, and planning artificial intelligence and automation opportunities for an organisation. The focus is on understanding the business processes that can benefit from automation rather than on selecting specific technologies or tools.

AI Automation Consultants typically work with businesses to understand their operations, systems, data, challenges, and goals. Some of the areas that are reviewed in the context of AI Automation Consulting include:

  • Customer service processes
  • Sales operations
  • Marketing activities
  • Finance functions
  • Human resources
  • Document-intensive processes
  • Internal reporting
  • Supply chain operations
  • Data management
  • Knowledge management
  • Software and system integrations

The aim is to identify the opportunities that would benefit from being automated and to define their priority.

A key principle of AI Automation Consulting is that the focus should be on the business problems that need to be addressed with automation, not on the tools or technologies.

Why UK-Based Businesses Need AI Automation Consulting

Organisations across the UK are experimenting with generative AI, AI agents, machine learning, intelligent automation, and large language models. However, experimentation alone is not enough, and businesses need to be able to connect their use cases for AI to tangible outcomes.

AI Automation Consulting enables organisations to make the right choices when it comes to adopting automation solutions.

Identify High-Impact AI Automation Opportunities

Most organisations have dozens of processes that could benefit from being automated. However, it is important to identify the opportunities that have the best potential to deliver value.

An AI automation opportunity may be defined by its potential to reduce costs, improve productivity, create capacity, enhance customer experiences, or realise other benefits. At the same time, the opportunity should have realistically achievable outcomes and should not require significant resources to implement.

AI automation consultants typically focus on processes that have the following characteristics:

  • High transaction volumes
  • Repetitive elements
  • Significant manual effort
  • High rates of information exchange
  • Predictable patterns
  • Lengthy processing times
  • High potential for errors
  • Multiple handoffs between systems
  • Large amounts of documentation
  • Repetitive decision-making

These are just some of the factors that should be considered when identifying high-impact automation opportunities.

Avoid Wrong AI Investments

Businesses have thousands of options when it comes to adopting AI automation solutions. However, it is important to ensure that the selected opportunities align with the organisation's needs and are realistically achievable.

Wrong AI investments often stem from organisations' attempts to purchase software or solutions without defining their business problems or opportunities. Some of the most common mistakes include:

  • Buying tools that are not used
  • Acquiring solutions that provide limited value
  • Purchasing systems that cannot be integrated
  • Automating processes with limited business impact
  • Making AI investments before preparing data
  • Failing to consider security requirements
  • Adopting AI solutions without defined ownership

AI Automation Consulting helps organisations to avoid these mistakes by focusing on their business requirements and opportunities rather than on available solutions and tools.

Define a Realistic AI Strategy

Many organisations that want to adopt AI solutions attempt to transform their operations at once. However, a more realistic approach is to identify a limited number of automation opportunities, implement them, and build on the results.

Most AI initiatives can be grouped into the following categories:

  • Quick wins — opportunities that deliver value with limited implementation complexity.
  • Strategic opportunities — larger initiatives that have the potential to realise significant benefits.
  • Long-term transformations — complex automation opportunities that typically require extensive preparation and support.

A comprehensive AI strategy should include an assessment of these factors.

How Should AI Automation Opportunities Be Prioritised?

An important aspect of AI Automation Consulting is the prioritisation of automation opportunities. Businesses need to consider several factors when prioritising AI opportunities, including:

Business Impact

The most important consideration is the potential impact that the automation opportunity can have on the business. This can include:

  • Cost reduction
  • Increased productivity
  • Higher capacity
  • Improved customer experiences
  • Reduced processing time
  • Additional revenue
  • Reduced employee effort

Opportunities with the highest potential impact should be prioritised.

AI Automation Feasibility

Some processes are more suitable for automation than others. Businesses should focus on opportunities that have realistic automation potential.

Processes that are highly structured and that have clearly defined input and output parameters are typically the easiest to automate. At the same time, organisations should avoid opportunities that require extensive human judgement.

Data Readiness

Most AI automation opportunities are heavily data-driven, and businesses need to consider how much data they have and how ready it is for analysis. Key considerations include:

  • Available information
  • Information location
  • Data quality
  • Data accessibility
  • Data permissions
  • Data freshness

These factors will influence the ability to realise value from AI automation.

Implementation Complexity

Some automation opportunities can be realised with limited complexity, while others require extensive changes to the organisation's operations. Businesses need to take implementation complexity into consideration when prioritising automation opportunities. Some of the factors that influence complexity include:

  • Availability of existing tools
  • Need for new APIs
  • Cloud infrastructure requirements
  • Data engineering requirements
  • Changes to business processes
  • Security requirements
  • Organisational changes

Complexity should be weighed against the potential benefits.

Security, Privacy, and Governance

Many businesses need to consider the impact of AI automation on data privacy, security, and governance. This is especially true for organisations that operate in highly regulated industries and that process sensitive information. Key considerations include:

  • Data privacy
  • Information security
  • Access controls
  • Human oversight
  • Accuracy and reliability
  • Transparency
  • Internal policies
  • Regulatory compliance

These factors should be carefully reviewed, especially for organisations that work with sensitive information.
