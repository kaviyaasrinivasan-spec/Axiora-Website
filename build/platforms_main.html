With Cargora, every shipment is AI-assisted — from document collection to final submission. Scroll through the full customs-clearance workflow below to see exactly what changes at every step.

The Manual Way

Business Impact
01Receive Import Documents

Clerk receives BL, Invoice & Packing List via WhatsApp/email and manually checks every file is present before starting.

AI

AI Document Checklist automatically verifies all mandatory documents are received & complete before processing begins.

90% fewer missing-document follow-ups
02Read & Extract Document Data

Clerk reads each PDF page by page and types values — container number, gross weight, shipper — into the ERP field by field.

AI

AI Vision Extraction automatically reads and extracts every key field from the documents in seconds.

70% faster data reading with higher accuracy
03Cross-Document Verification

Clerk opens the BL, Commercial Invoice & Packing List side by side and compares values one by one, by eye.

AI

AI Cross-Verification compares all linked fields across every document — and understands that 1.5 MT = 1500 KGS.

Zero missed discrepancies · 100% audit-ready
04Bill of Entry Preparation

Clerk manually fills each BOE field, copying values from separate documents — prone to copy errors & missing fields.

AI

AI BOE Preparation extracts verified data and auto-fills the Bill of Entry, ready for review.

70% faster BOE filing with higher accuracy
05IGM & Inward Data Matching

Clerk logs into ICEGATE, searches the IGM number, then copies the IGM & inward dates back to match the shipment.

AI

Fetch ICEGATE — one click pulls the IGM number, inward date & port data directly from ICEGATE and matches it.

Zero manual portal searches · instant matching
06Format & Date Conversion

Clerk reformats dates (DD/MM/YYYY → YYYY/MM/DD) and converts units (MT → KGS) by hand for portal submission.

AI

AI Data Transformation applies pre-configured format & unit-conversion rules automatically on every extraction.

100% format compliance · zero portal rejections
07Repeated Extraction Errors

The same extraction mistake repeats on every similar document, and the clerk must correct it every single time.

AI

AI Training Loop — the clerk corrects it once, and Cargora remembers it permanently for every future document.

The same error never happens twice
08Management Reporting & Review

Manager copies figures into Excel and compiles a weekly summary report that takes several hours to prepare.

AI

AI Dashboard shows real-time insights — TAT per shipment, cost per entry, team performance & exception alerts.

Better decisions · hours of reporting saved
09ERP & Portal Data Entry

Clerk types each value into the ERP/portal field by field — 1–3 hours of typing that is highly prone to typos.

AI

Cargora RPA reads all extracted values and fills every ERP/portal field instantly — no manual typing.

80% less data-entry time · zero typo errors
10Submission & Confirmation

Staff watches the screen during submission and manually records the result in a register to track completion.

AI

A headless background bot submits on the server while the operator is free — with an instant success / error alert.

Operator walks away · notified automatically when done
