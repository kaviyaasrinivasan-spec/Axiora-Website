About the Founder

Senthil Pitchai

Founder & CEO
20+ years in enterprise architecture

Axiora Labs was founded by Senthil Pitchai, an Enterprise Solution Architect with over 20 years of experience building large-scale enterprise systems.

After years of designing high-performance architectures for enterprises, Senthil noticed something surprising — while technology was advancing rapidly, many business operations were still manual, repetitive, and inefficient.

"AI is not the future. It is the next necessary shift in how businesses operate."

In August 2025, he transformed that belief into action and founded Axiora Labs — with a mission to automate real-world business workflows using enterprise-grade architecture and intelligent systems.
